# Pricing recommendation

**Proceed with a staged rollout.**

Moving from $29 to $32 changed 90-day contribution per assigned visitor by $+0.68 (95% CI $+0.42 to $+0.94).

| Metric | $29 control | $32 treatment | Change (95% CI) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Contribution per visitor | $5.38 | $6.06 | $+0.68 ($+0.42, $+0.94) |
| Conversion | 18.62% | 16.94% | -1.68 pp (-2.42, -0.93) |
| 90-day retained per visitor | 12.78% | 11.09% | -1.69 pp (-2.32, -1.06) |
| 90-day retention among buyers | 68.63% | 65.44% | Descriptive |

The illustrative rollout rule requires a positive lower confidence bound on contribution lift and lower bounds above −2.5 percentage points for both conversion and retained visitors. Ramp gradually and continue monitoring conversion and 90-day retained visitors.

Buyer-only retention is descriptive because the price can change who subscribes. These results come from simulated behavior and do not predict a real pricing outcome.
